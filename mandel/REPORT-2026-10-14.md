## What Mandel learned, in one paragraph each

**From your resources.** Information is physical and has a cost, so every learning
action is budgeted and every claimed edge pays its bill (01). Fields that adopt
pre-registration late produce small positive effects under weak controls, which is
the shape of self-deception Mandel must design against (02, 03). Self-improvement
without a ruler the optimiser cannot touch is noise; the ruler is the hard part
(04). Learning is minimising a frozen cost on data the learner has not seen,
scored in bits (05, 07). A knowledge base compounds only if it is itemised,
append-only, routed from a short index, and audited (06). Iterated maps converge
only under contraction, and the attractor belongs to the maps, not the seed (08,
09, 10). Markov chains are the null model for anything that claims to predict a
sequence (11). An LLM designs and interprets; deterministic code computes,
executes and holds the risk limits; the human holds the live-money switch (12, 13,
14). Roughness is a measured exponent with a scale range, never an adjective, and
the Mandelbrot set's real gift is the distinction between parameter space and state
space, plus renormalisation as the honest form of self-similarity (15, 16).

**From what Mandel found.** The Darwin Gödel Machine is the closest working
blueprint: an archive with lineage, cheap-first staged evaluation, and the lesson
that the agent will edit its own detector if it can reach it (A1). AlphaEvolve sets
the precondition, a machine-gradable evaluator, and the evaluation cascade (A2).
Agentic Context Engineering shows how a knowledge context improves without
collapsing: itemised bullets with helpful/harmful counters, incremental deltas,
never a wholesale rewrite (A3). Mandelbrot's own market model turns "fractal
markets" into a falsifiable scaling test, and its estimable successor (the
Markov-switching multifractal) beats GARCH at long horizons (A4). The best
published LLM forecaster still loses to the crowd overall and wins only where the
market is uncertain and the information is fresh; the market price is the
benchmark (A5).

**The design that came out of it.** Mandel's binding constraint is resolved
out-of-sample observations, not reasoning. So the loop is a deterministic,
zero-token daily job that accrues observations, builds evidence from records
written before outcomes were known, and decides proposals on the day they become
decidable; a model session starts only to interpret a decision or a report. Most
days end at zero model tokens. The ruler sits under a hash manifest with git
history as the external anchor; controls (a coin, an oracle, a shuffle null) run
beside every scored source so a leak shows up as a coin with skill.

## Where Mandel was wrong, and said so

- Four source files (07, 08, 14, 16) were initially written by Mandel itself when
  delegated learners were blocked by usage limits; they were replaced by full
  learner files once credits returned, and the critique names the ones still thin.
- The first engine review found that the deflated Sharpe ratio applied no
  deflation unless trial variances were supplied; the second found that every
  proposal transition trusted its file copy over the ledger. Both are fixed with
  regression tests. The lesson is already a principle: the gate must be recomputed
  from records, not read from a file.
- Five passages of the synthesis overclaimed or misattributed; they were corrected
  the same day and the critique that found them is in the tree.

## Readiness for meeting the children

Mandel is ready to be introduced. The protocol (MANDEL.md section 5): read before
guiding; register with session id, domain and a paragraph; record a first report;
turn claims into candidate insights with provenance `child:<name>:report:<id>`;
only then draft guidance, and only from validated insights or the standard
failure-mode checklist. Every child is scored on the same forecast book as Mandel
and the market.

What Mandel needs from you at first contact:

1. For each child, the session (or repository) to read, and what it is for.
2. A name for each, or a choice from `python -m mandel names` (Julia, Fatou,
   Douady, Hubbard, Barnsley, Sierpinski, Koch, Cantor, Hausdorff, Hurst, …).
3. Whether the children may write forecasts into the shared book directly
   (recommended: a daily batch per child), or report through Mandel.

The first measurements Mandel will ask each child for: resolved forecasts with the
market price at forecast time; a trade log with fees and fills; the honest count of
variants tried; the held-out windows used. Nothing a child claims becomes
validated knowledge until it has passed the gate.

## Cost of the genesis cycle

Recorded in the ledger (`python -m mandel cycles`): about 165 delegated agent
launches across 17 workflow runs (roughly a third of them failed on usage limits
and were re-run), about 8.6 million subagent tokens, and about five hours of
wall-clock. Outcomes: 21 sources learned, 696 candidate insights, 0 proposals yet.
The gains-per-cost ratio is therefore zero by Mandel's own definition, which is the
honest baseline: genesis buys the ruler; the first validated gains come from the
first cycles that run the loop.
