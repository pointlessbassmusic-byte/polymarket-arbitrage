# Proposal 1: The Renormalisation Ladder

*Architect 1 of 4. Lens: fractal / renormalisation. Written 2026-10-14 against engine v0.1.0 (`mandel/`, 79 tests), MANDEL.md v0, SYNTHESIS v0 and ORIGINAL-IDEAS v0. Everything here is a design proposal; each component becomes a pre-registered proposal before it is built, and the fractal vocabulary is a naming convention, not evidence.*

## 1. Thesis

One loop shape (observe, hypothesise, pre-register, measure, gate, record) runs at three nested levels, strategy, child and Mandel, with one record schema (`Proposal`, `Criterion`, evidence dict, gate verdict, region) at every level; what differs between levels (cadence, budget, thresholds, minimum sample) is derived from measurement at that level and logged, never copied from the level above. The Mandelbrot set is used for exactly two things: it is a catalogue of *parameter space* kept apart from *state space*, so the gate's knowledge is stored as regions of settings with a centre, a root, a margin and a boundary rather than as a best point; and its small copies are homeomorphic to the whole but not similar, so a sub-loop inherits the parent's gate only after a straightening check and is expected to need its own decorations.

## 2. The levels

| Level | Unit | Cadence | A proposal changes | Evidence | Who acts |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| L0 forecast stream | one question | per question | nothing: record, then score | resolution; Brier; `skill_vs_market` | children, Mandel, `market:*` feed |
| L1 strategy | a parameterised component: `ArbConfig.min_edge`, `min_spread`, `taker_fee_bps`, `slippage_tolerance`, `min_match_similarity`, `RiskConfig` limits, or a child's model | when `min_n` out-of-sample observations have accrued on the target; checked by the daily job | parameters inside an evolve-block | walk-forward, block bootstrap, DSR; or Brier delta on held-out questions | a child or Mandel drafts; the gate decides |
| L2 child | a child's process: its checklists, prompt version, measurement protocol, its `LevelSpec` | weekly | the child's procedure | L1 pass rate and cost per accepted L1 proposal over the next *n* L1 decisions | Mandel drafts; the gate decides |
| L3 Mandel | Mandel's own procedure: proposer version, ingest rules, budgets, synthesis regeneration; MANDEL.md with the user | monthly; MANDEL.md quarterly | Mandel's files outside `validation/` | per-level efficiency, straightening failures, idea-market Brier, proposer hit rate | Mandel drafts; the gate decides; ruler changes carry `actor="human"` |

L0 is deliberately not a loop. A forecast has no hypothesis, no criteria and no gate; it is recorded and scored. SYNTHESIS's verdict on ORIGINAL-IDEAS 7 is adopted as written: three levels loop and the fourth feeds them. The level that was missing is upward: the proposer is a versioned component whose hit rate is an L3 observation (A1, Hyperagents).

## 3. Components

Each item names the module it extends. Nothing under `validation/` changes without `actor="human"`.

**3.1 `mandel/levels.py` (new), `LevelSpec`, `mandel/state/levels.json`.** One record per level: `{level, cadence, budget: {agents, api_calls, wall_seconds, tokens_est}, evidence_kind, resolution_source, min_n_rule, criteria_set, straightening: {domain, properness, degree, checked_at, result}, derived_from: [cycle ids], as_of}`. Budget fields are exactly `cycles.COST_FIELDS`, so `CycleLog.efficiency()` can be computed per level without a new unit. Every change to a budget is a ledger event `level.budget` with the numbers it was derived from.

**3.2 `Proposal` gains six fields** (`proposals.py`): `level` (0..3; `draft` refuses level 0), `params` (the settings tried, as `{name: value}` on a declared grid), `critical_metric`, `stage_reached` (0..3), `proposer_version` (hash of the proposer checklist file in force), `pass_forecast_id` (see 3.7). `compute_criteria_hash` is unchanged, so existing ledger events still verify. `target` keeps its three forms; the level is redundant with the target prefix only at L3, which is why it is stored, not inferred.

**3.3 Region catalogue: `mandel/regions.py` (new), `mandel/state/regions.jsonl`.** `ParameterRegion {id, target, level, grid: {name: step}, box: {name: [lo, hi]}, centre: {name: value}, root: proposal_id, root_kind: "primitive"|"satellite", multiplier: float in [0,1), boundary: [proposal_id], mechanism: str|null, holdout_ids, as_of, status}`. The multiplier is the normalised gate margin (`1 - min_dsr/dsr` for strategies, Brier skill over the market for forecasters); `root` is the accepted proposal the region grew from, `satellite` when its `params` lie inside an existing box. `ProposalBook.decide` calls `regions.update(proposal, gate_result)`: an accept with `params` extends or creates a region, a reject joins the nearest region's `boundary`. A region with `mechanism=null` is a ghost and the insights it validates are capped at `SELF_ONLY_CAP` (16, "ghost components"). `region_map(target)` returns regions, gaps and boundaries. The catalogue is real at L1, where the bot's numeric settings in `config.yaml` are a genuine parameter space; at L2 and L3 the parameters are discrete and regions degenerate to points (section 9).

**3.4 Straightening check** (`levels.py`, `straighten(child_spec, parent_spec, proposal)`): three conditions, logged as `level.straightened` or `level.straightening_failed`. *Domain*: the sub-level's data window and budget are a proper subset of the parent's. *Properness*: each sub-proposal maps to exactly one parent observation (one L1 decision is one row in the L2 evidence; one L2 decision is one row in L3's). *Degree*: exactly one `critical_metric` is declared. If all three hold, the sub-level inherits the parent's criteria set from `gate.py`; if any fails, the level gets its own written criteria set and the failure is counted. Metrics: `renormalisation_depth` (nested levels that passed) and `straightening_failures`. This is ORIGINAL-IDEAS 7's falsification clause made operational.

**3.5 Critical-orbit cascade** (`validation/gate.py`, `validation/backtest_eval.py`; human-edited). Each L1 target declares one `critical_metric` and a pre-registered `escape_radius`. Stage 1 runs only the critical metric on the first walk-forward fold (for the bot: net PnL per opportunity at 2x the configured friction; for a forecaster: rolling `skill_vs_market`). A candidate that escapes stops and is logged with `escape_time` (folds survived). Stage 2 is a short slice; stage 3 is full walk-forward, `block_bootstrap_pvalue` and `deflated_sharpe_ratio`. `stage_reached` is recorded on the proposal. This is A1/A2's cheap-first cascade with Hubbard's diagnostic named; the fractal content is only that one cheap trajectory decides membership before the expensive computation.

**3.6 `validation/scales.py` (new, human-edited) and `n_scales_agree`.** Coarse-grain the held-out evidence at declared aggregations (per question, per category, per week, per month, per platform), keep the aggregations with `n_eff >= 8`, and count those where the sign of the edge agrees with the headline. `Criterion("n_scales_agree", ">=", k)` joins `forecast_improvement_criteria` and `strategy_improvement_criteria` with *k* frozen at pre-registration (default 2 of the available scales). The coarse scales are aggregates of the same data, so this is a filter against single-scale artefacts, not independent confirmation (SYNTHESIS verdict on idea 3). It is the one place where the fractal idea touches data, and it runs under its own falsification test (section 12).

**3.7 Idea market** (`forecasts.py`, `proposals.py`). Before `preregister`, Mandel records `ForecastBook.record(source="mandel:self", question_id=f"proposal:{id}:passes", p=..., market_p=<base rate of accepts for that level and target prefix at forecast time>)`; the id is stored in `pass_forecast_id` and `preregister` refuses without it, so the forecast cannot adapt to the frozen criteria. `decide` resolves the question. Because `market_p` is self-reported, `score_source("mandel:self")` labels the reference as such; the number that matters is `skill_vs_market` against the base rate, with coverage (fraction of proposals forecast) beside it (ORIGINAL-IDEAS 2, A5's selective-forecasting caveat). Proposer hit rate per `proposer_version` is a view over the same rows.

**3.8 Level-aware cycles and budget derivation** (`cycles.py`). `Cycle.level` is added; `efficiency(level=k)` returns gains per unit cost for that level. Seeds: L1 proposal <= 1 agent, 30k `tokens_est`, 600 `wall_seconds`, 50 `api_calls`, at most two open proposals per target; L2 cycle <= 2 agents, 150k `tokens_est`, one process proposal per child per week; L3 cycle <= 600k `tokens_est`, at most two proposals per month; L0 <= 200 `api_calls` per day inside the daily job. After each L3 cycle, `budget_k(next) = clip(median cost per accepted proposal at level k * target_accepts_k, 0.5 * current, 2 * current)`; a level with zero accepts keeps its budget. Domain rule: the budgets a level hands down cannot sum to more than it was given. Costs remain operator estimates, which `cycles.py` already labels.

**3.9 Exterior map and CLI** (`cli.py`, `graph.py`). `python -m mandel regions [--target]` prints `region_map`; `python -m mandel exterior` runs weekly over rejected proposals and reports, per target, the potential `G = log(required_margin / achieved_margin)` and the stage at which each failed, so Mandel can see how far from acceptance the rejects sit and which failure mode dominates. `graph.py` gains one node class, `region`, linked to the proposals and insights it validates. Both commands only read files.

**3.10 Children protocol** (`children.py`). `ChildReport` gains `level`, `regions: [ParameterRegion dict]`, `escape_stats`, `critical_metric`; `Guidance` gains `level_spec` (the child's budget, critical metric and straightening status) and `regions` (validated regions touching its domain). Reports remain append-only; everything a child claims still enters as a candidate insight with provenance `child:<name>:report:<id>`.

## 4. Data flow

L0 forecasts and `market:*` prices enter the forecast book; the daily job resolves questions and refreshes the scoreboard. L1 proposals draw their evidence from resolved questions (forecasters) or from the bot's dry-run `opportunities.log` and `trades.log` replayed through `compare_strategies` with a declared fee and fill model (strategies); accepted L1 proposals write regions. A child's weekly report carries its L1 decisions, regions and escape statistics; those rows are L2's observations, and an L2 proposal's evidence is the change in L1 pass rate and cost per accept over the next *n* L1 decisions. L2 decisions, straightening results, per-level efficiency, idea-market scores and proposer hit rates are L3's observations; L3 proposals change Mandel's procedure, and re-derived budgets flow back down as `LevelSpec` updates and guidance. Every write at every level is a ledger event; the hash chain is the only memory shared by the levels.

## 5. One cycle end to end

*L1, strategy, the bot's `min_edge`.* The daily job (OPERATIONS.md timer, extended with `forecast-resolve` and `levels check`) finds that the target has accrued `min_n` new dry-run periods since its last decision and opens a slot. A child or Mandel reads `region_map("repo:core/arb_engine.py")`, picks a setting one grid step outside the current box (or a literature-transfer idea), records the pass forecast, drafts with `params`, `critical_metric="pnl_per_opp_2x_friction"`, `n_trials_declared` and criteria `strategy_improvement_criteria() + [n_scales_agree >= 2]`, plus `n_holdout_windows >= 2` if the setting is within one grid step of a boundary entry, and pre-registers. The cascade runs; the gate writes evidence from its own output; `decide` records the verdict, resolves the pass forecast, updates the region and moves helpful/harmful counters on `depends_on_insights`. Cost is noted on the open L1 cycle.

*L2, a child, weekly.* Mandel reads the child's report, files candidate insights, computes the child's L1 pass rate and cost per accept, and drafts one process proposal ("adding the point-in-time checklist before every backtest raises this child's L1 pass rate out of sample") with criteria over the next ten L1 decisions. Evidence accrues from the ledger; nothing is re-run.

*L3, Mandel, monthly.* Observations: `efficiency(level=k)` per level, `straightening_failures`, idea-market skill and coverage, proposer hit rate per version, `error_scaling_slope` (held-out error against cumulative evidence in log-log coordinates, from 15). One or two proposals about Mandel's own procedure; budgets re-derived and logged; SYNTHESIS regenerated from the store; MANDEL.md changes drafted here and decided with the user.

## 6. Validation

The gate is unchanged in kind: `evaluate` applies frozen criteria to evidence, with the structural checks already in code. What is added: a fourth standard set, `process_improvement_criteria(min_decisions=10)` = `n_decisions >= 10`, `pass_rate_delta > 0`, `cost_per_accept_delta < 0`, for L2 and L3; `n_scales_agree` in the two existing sets; `n_holdout_windows >= 2` when the boundary flag is set; `stage_reached == 3` as a precondition for any accept at L1. New evaluators, all under `validation/` and human-edited: `scales.py` (coarse-graining), the stage-1 critical-orbit runner in `backtest_eval.py`, a `process_eval.py` that computes the L2/L3 metrics from ledger rows, and a boundary-distance function in `regions.py` that the gate calls but cannot be changed by a proposal. The protected-ruler manifest from SYNTHESIS design principle 1 is a precondition for all of this and is listed in week 1.

## 7. Child integration

A child is an L2 unit with its own L1 targets. First contact follows MANDEL.md section 5; the additions are that Mandel writes the child a `LevelSpec` (budget, critical metric, cadence), runs the straightening check, and tells the child in guidance whether it inherits Mandel's criteria sets or must write its own. Children never run the gate on Mandel's behalf: they report decisions, regions and raw evidence paths; Mandel's gate re-computes anything that enters a region. Remote-session tools, when granted, carry only guidance and report requests; the record is the repo. Shared questions among children are pooled only after the effective rank of their error matrix is measured (SYNTHESIS tension on redundancy).

## 8. Frugality

Budgets are per level in the existing cost fields and are compared with measured gains every L3 cycle; stage 1 of the cascade is designed to kill most L1 candidates at under a tenth of full cost; L2 and L3 evidence is counted from the ledger, never re-run; `regions`, `exterior` and `levels check` are file reads; the daily job stays maintenance-only. Not done: no loop at L0; no fixed budget ratio between levels; no regions for targets with fewer than two decisions; no vector store or database; no LLM judge anywhere near the gate; no live-money change; no re-running a child's backtests except where a region would be written.

## 9. Where self-similarity is real and where it is forced

*Real and useful.* The record schema is identical at L1, L2 and L3, so the ledger, `verify`, `reconcile`, the idea market and the counters work unchanged at every level; this is 09's IFS lesson (seed-independent rules) and it costs nothing. Parameter space is kept apart from state space and the gate's knowledge is stored as regions with boundary data (16); for the bot's numeric settings this is a genuine map. The straightening check is the honest form of inheritance: shape is copied, decorations are re-derived (homeomorphic, not similar). The progress metric has the same shape at every level: the contraction ratio of held-out error per accepted proposal (09), with a confidence interval.

*Forced, and labelled so.* A fixed budget ratio between levels (ORIGINAL-IDEAS 7 as first written) is replaced by derivation. L0 as a loop is dropped. Regions at L2 and L3 are points with a fancy name. Potentials and angles in the exterior map are a sorting convention for rejects, not a theory. `n_scales_agree` is a coarse-graining filter on one data set, not a multifractal test; A4's partition-function test is a separate, later proposal. Nothing here says markets are fractal; the only data-level scaling claims Mandel will make are `ScalingEstimate` records with estimator, range, interval and a beaten null (15, 09), and none are needed for this architecture to run.

## 10. First four weeks

*Week 1.* Protected-ruler manifest (`validation/MANIFEST.json`, hash checked at `preregister` and `evaluate`, human-edited). `levels.py` with `LevelSpec`, seeded `levels.json`, `straighten()`. The six `Proposal` fields and `Cycle.level`, with tests. Run `python -m mandel ingest`. Begin recording `market:polymarket` prices for a fixed set of 50 questions in the daily job so L0 has data.

*Week 2.* `regions.py`, `state/regions.jsonl`, the `decide` hook, `cli regions`. `validation/scales.py` and `n_scales_agree`. Stage-1 critical-orbit runner and `stage_reached`. First L1 proposal on `min_edge` from replayed dry-run logs with a declared fee model, pre-registered with the full criteria set.

*Week 3.* Idea market: `pass_forecast_id` enforced at `preregister`, base-rate reference, scoreboard view. `process_improvement_criteria` and `process_eval.py`. First child registered under the first-contact protocol; straightening check run and logged; first L2 proposal drafted. `cli exterior`.

*Week 4.* First L3 mini-cycle: `efficiency(level=k)` for each level, budgets re-derived and logged as `level.budget`, SYNTHESIS regenerated from the store, this document's predictions checked and the results filed as insights with provenance `proposal:<id>` or `mandel:self`.

## 11. Risks

Resolution rates are slow: ten L1 decisions per child may take a month, so L2 criteria may wait a quarter and L3 longer. Coarse scales have small *n*, so `n_scales_agree` may reject real edges; *k* must be set modestly. A dozen settings on a coarse grid still give a sparse region map. Budget derivation runs on operator-estimated costs; the 0.5x to 2x clip bounds the damage. With one child, the L2 machinery is overhead. The fractal vocabulary can lure a session into forcing structure; section 9 and the falsification tests are the guard. Children sharing a base model and data are correlated, so their agreement is one source until measured. A ghost region can pass the gate with no mechanism; the 0.5 cap limits propagation but does not stop adoption in dry run.

## 12. Falsifiable predictions

1. By week 4 the straightening check passes for L1 under L2 and fails at least one condition for L0 under L1 (L0 lacks a critical hypothesis). If L0 passes all three, the design has under-counted levels and L0 should become a loop.
2. Within eight weeks stage 1 rejects at least 30% of L1 candidates at under 10% of full-evaluation cost. If stage 1 rejects nothing that stage 3 would not have, the cascade is cost without protection and is reduced to sampling.
3. Within the first L3 cycle, at least one level's re-derived budget differs from its geometric seed by more than a factor of two. If all three stay within the seed ratio over two L3 cycles, the fixed ratio was adequate and derivation is bookkeeping.
4. Among the first ten boundary-flagged L1 proposals, at least one verdict flips on the extra window. If `boundary_flip_rate` is zero over twenty, the extra window is dropped.
5. Among the first twenty L1 proposals, `n_scales_agree` rejects at least one that every other criterion passed; longer term, multi-scale survivors fail their next out-of-sample window less often than single-scale survivors would have (ORIGINAL-IDEAS 3). If they fail as often, the criterion is removed.
6. After twenty decided L1 proposals, at least one target has a region with a non-empty boundary or two regions. If every region is a single point, the grid is too fine or proposals are too scattered to learn from, and the catalogue is downgraded to a log.
7. The pass forecast is recorded before pre-registration for 100% of proposals (procedural, checkable from the ledger). The slow test, Brier skill over the class base rate positive after fifty decisions with coverage not falling, is registered now and read later.
8. `efficiency(level=k)` differs by level: gains per `tokens_est` at L1 exceed L2 and L3 in the first quarter, because L1 has the most resolutions. If L3 shows the highest ratio early, that is a sign L3 proposals are too easy, and `process_improvement_criteria` is tightened.
