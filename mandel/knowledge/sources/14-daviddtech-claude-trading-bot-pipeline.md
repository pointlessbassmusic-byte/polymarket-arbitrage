## Evidence quality

**Rating: promotional, with a practitioner-anecdotal core.** Reasons:

- *Incentive structure.* The data source (TradingView), the backtester (TradingKit/Trader.dev), the execution bridge (trigger.trade, free only for Skool members), the strategy library (Strategy Factory) and the community are all the creator's products or funnels. The video is a product demo in the shape of a tutorial. That does not make the five rules wrong (they are standard and correct), but every number in it is marketing.
- *The headline claim is unverifiable and, where testable, inconsistent.* "+102.5% in about a month" is a single account, self-reported, with no drawdown, trade log, or fee-inclusive statement recovered; the public scoreboard could not be fetched. The leaderboard numbers on the creator's own site do not reconcile: 735 trades in 7 days on a 45-minute chart is more trades than there are bars (7 × 24 × 60 / 45 = 224), so the bot is pyramiding, averaging down (Botify has a DCA mode), or the window is not 7 days. Trustpilot complaints about advertised returns of 17,000–25,000% describe a shop that has published backtest numbers as if they were results before.
- *Selection at scale, presented as rigour.* 1,368 tests → 277 "passed" → 7 traded, "2,000+ strategies tested", "352,000 community backtests". The creator treats the pass count as evidence; it is the opposite. With `N` zero-skill trials the expected best Sharpe is `E[max SR] ≈ √V · [(1−γ)·Z⁻¹(1−1/N) + γ·Z⁻¹(1−1/(Ne))]` (Bailey & López de Prado 2014; implemented in `mandel/validation/backtest_eval.py::expected_max_sharpe`). For `N = 1,368` the bracket is about 3.3, so the best pure-noise candidate sits 3.3 standard errors above zero before anyone has found an edge. Harvey & Liu (2015) make the same point for t-statistics: after hundreds of trials a nominal `t = 3.0` deflates to an adjusted `t ≈ 2.0`, and the haircut is non-linear (marginal Sharpes are "heavily penalized"). Nothing in the recovered material applies a multiple-testing correction.
- *Live evidence is tiny.* Four scalps worth $15; one month of one account. Neither can distinguish skill from a trending crypto market in 2026.
- *A subtle look-ahead the creator does not name.* [mine] An LLM that "finds" a strategy on BTC/USDT 2023–2026 has read commentary about that period. Rules it proposes can encode knowledge of which regimes trended, which is a leak from the test window into the model-selection step that no train/test split inside the data can remove. The only clean test is incubation on data that did not exist when the strategy was written, which is exactly rule 5, so the creator's practice is better than his explanation.
- *What is sound.* The five rules; judging on PF/MDD/trade count against buy-and-hold rather than win rate; stops on the exchange rather than in the alerting layer; sub-account caps; a monitor that compares live statistics with the backtest's expected range and can disable automation; the separation of LLM-as-researcher from code-as-executor. These are standard practitioner hygiene and agree with resources 12 and 13.

## What Mandel adopts

1. **LLM as researcher and monitor, never as the live decision-maker.** Because the only parts of the pipeline that are auditable, testable and switch-off-able are the deterministic strategy and the executor; the agent's value is in the search and the watching (same conclusion as resource 12).
2. **Cost-inclusive evaluation as a hard requirement of the gate.** Because `net edge = e − c` and `c` scales with trade count; a candidate evaluated at zero fees and mid-price fills has not been evaluated. Mandel already carries venue fees in `config.yaml` (`taker_fee_bps: 150`, `maker_fee_bps: 0`, `slippage_tolerance: 0.02`); the gate must refuse evidence that was not produced with them.
3. **Profit factor, max drawdown, trade count and a naive benchmark as the reported quartet, not win rate.** Because win rate is trivially gamed by stop/target geometry and PF already encodes `p`, `W` and `L`.
4. **Incubation as a gate stage that cannot be contaminated by selection.** Because it is the one out-of-sample test that best-of-N search over history cannot reach: the data did not exist when the candidate was chosen. Mandel's principle 13 (no live-money change without the gate and the user) already requires dry-run; this makes the dry-run window a measured, pre-registered stage with its own pass criteria.
5. **"Expected range" monitoring with a pre-registered kill switch.** Because the follow-up's "losing streak vs edge decay" is the correct question and it is answerable: derive the distribution of drawdowns, streaks and rolling PF from the accepted candidate's own backtest, and demote or disable when live falls outside it.
6. **Selection-aware acceptance: every search declares its trial count.** Because 1,368 → 277 is noise unless deflated. Mandel's `Proposal.n_trials_declared` and `deflated_sharpe_ratio(n_trials=...)` exist for this; the lesson is that the count must include every variant the agent tried, including ones it discarded before reporting.
7. **No signal may read information that was not available at decision time (the multi-timeframe rule, generalised).** Because repainting is look-ahead, and in Mandel's domain the analogues are using a later order-book snapshot, a resolved outcome, or a cross-venue quote that arrived after the decision timestamp.
8. **Risk limits enforced in the executor, not in the alerting or signal layer.** Because stops that live in TradingView fail during wicks; limits that live in a notification pipeline fail when the pipeline lags. Mandel's position caps (`max_position_per_market`, `max_order_size`) must be checked at order placement, not upstream.
9. **Capped, isolated capital per strategy.** Because "if the AI does something unexpected, the damage is capped at that number" is the correct design for anything new, and Mandel's children are new.
10. **Diversification measured as correlation, not counted as bots.** Because "non-correlated" is a matrix property; seven bots on one factor are one bot.
11. **A public ledger of every live decision.** Because the one thing the creator does that improves on his genre is to promise profits, losses, drawdowns and open trades in public; Mandel's append-only `ledger.jsonl` is that scoreboard, and it must include losses.
12. **Research from the commons first.** Because converting an existing, documented indicator or published rule into a testable candidate is cheaper and more honest than inventing one; Mandel's adjacent-resource and children pipelines are its version of "paste the open-source script into Claude".

## What Mandel holds loosely or rejects

- **Reject the profitability claims as evidence of anything.** "+102.5%" is one month of one self-reported account from a vendor whose site has advertised four- and five-digit percentage returns; no fee-inclusive statement, drawdown or trade log was recovered. Mandel records the number as a claim with provenance "vendor, unverified", never as a prior.
- **Reject pass counts as evidence.** "277 of 1,368 passed" is the expected output of running 1,368 noisy tests against a threshold on a trending market; without the trial-level distribution it says nothing about the 277.
- **Hold loosely: "volatility is forecastable, direction isn't."** True as a rule of thumb (volatility clusters; returns are close to unpredictable), and it motivates regime filters that Mandel's HMM work (resource 13) also uses. But a volatility filter that doubled the in-sample PF and halved the trade count was *chosen because it did that*, which is one more trial in the count.
- **Hold loosely: the NNFX template as a strategy prior.** It is a sane template for trend-following on continuous price series. Prediction-market contracts are bounded, resolve to 0/1, and are priced as probabilities; moving-average trend logic transfers poorly. What transfers is the *structure*: one direction signal, independent confirmations, a filter for the regime in which the signal is known to fail, and exits defined before entry.
- **Hold loosely: "incubate for one to three months."** The right incubation length is a trade-count and confidence-interval question, not a calendar one; for a strategy with a handful of trades per week, three months is still too few trades to separate PF 1.3 from PF 1.0.
- **Reject the single-timeframe rule as stated; keep its reason.** Multi-timeframe logic is fine if every input is lagged to what was knowable at the decision bar. The rule exists because most implementations are not, and because it is hard to audit; Mandel adopts the audit, not the ban.
- **Reject the "no coding required" framing for Mandel's own work.** The video's audience is retail; Mandel's learning lives in versioned code and evaluation harnesses, and a candidate whose code Mandel cannot read is the black box the creator says nearly wiped him out.
- **Reject LLM-run live scalping entirely** (the sibling's 4-for-4 experiment), including as a demo: tiny sample, 9× leverage, and the creator himself says the AI's trading instincts are the losing crowd's.
- **Hold the "AI Trading Arena" and LLM-vs-LLM leaderboards at arm's length.** They are entertainment, not evaluation: no shared costs, no shared risk limits, short windows, survivorship of the loud.

## Design implications for the RSI predictive substrate

Concrete changes and additions, phrased against the existing code in `mandel/`:

1. **Cost-inclusive evidence is mandatory.** Extend `gate.strategy_improvement_criteria()` and `evidence_from_comparison()` so evidence carries `costs_included == 1.0`, `fee_model_id` (hash of the venue fee/slippage parameters used) and `fill_model` (`"mid"` is rejected; `"book_depth"` or `"conservative_touch"` accepted). A comparison whose returns were computed at mid-price or zero fee fails the gate before any statistic is read. Slippage for the arb bot is depth-weighted: `fill_price = Σ_k p_k·q_k / Σ_k q_k` over the book levels needed to fill `q`, not the best quote.

2. **Report the quartet.** Every backtest record stores `pf_net`, `max_drawdown`, `n_trades`, `benchmark_return` (for the arb bot: holding the market price to resolution, or doing nothing) alongside Sharpe. Add `Criterion("n_trades", ">=", 100)` as a default for strategy proposals; below that, PF confidence intervals are too wide to act on (at `p = 0.5`, 100 trades gives a standard error of 0.05 on the win rate alone).

3. **Declare the whole search.** When a child or Mandel searches strategy variants, the search harness, not the author, counts trials: every parameter set, indicator swap and filter evaluated increments `n_trials`, which is written to `Proposal.n_trials_declared` and consumed by `deflated_sharpe_ratio`. A proposal whose declared trial count is lower than the harness's log fails integrity (`check_integrity`). This converts "1,368 tests, 277 passed" from a boast into a deflation factor.

4. **Incubation as a pre-registered gate stage.** Add a lifecycle state between `decided` and `live`: `incubating`, with a pre-registered window defined in trades, not days (`min_incubation_trades`, default 100, or fewer only if the decision rule is pre-registered), recorded against the hypothetical fills of the dry-run executor using the same cost model as the backtest. Pass criteria pre-registered at `preregister()`: live `pf_net` above a floor derived from the backtest's bootstrap 10th percentile; live drawdown inside the backtest's 95th-percentile drawdown; live trade frequency within ±50% of the backtest's (a frequency mismatch means the signal is firing on different conditions than it was tested on, i.e. a repaint or data mismatch). Incubating candidates are never re-ranked and re-selected against each other on their incubation results alone; the incubation decision is per-candidate against its own pre-registered thresholds, otherwise incubation becomes one more selection step.

5. **Expected-range monitoring with a kill switch (the risk agent, done as code).** For each live strategy, derive from its accepted backtest: the trade-level win rate `p` and P&L distribution; bootstrap `B` equity paths of length `n` (block bootstrap over trades, `block_bootstrap_pvalue` already exists) to get the 5th-percentile equity, the 95th-percentile drawdown and the 99th-percentile losing streak (`P(streak ≥ k in n trades) ≈ n·(1−p)^k`). A `monitor` loop compares the live window with these bands and writes a ledger event on each breach. Pre-registered actions: one band breached → `demote` (size ÷ 2, alert the user); two bands or the equity floor → `disable` (executor refuses new orders for that strategy; open positions managed by the executor's own stops). Re-enabling is a new proposal through the gate. The LLM may be asked to *investigate* a breach and draft the post-mortem, but the breach detection and the disable are deterministic.

6. **Decision-time data discipline.** Every feature a signal reads carries an `available_at` timestamp; the backtest harness refuses features with `available_at > decision_ts`. For the arb bot this covers book snapshots, cross-venue quotes and any resolution or settlement information. This is the generalisation of "no multi-timeframe" and of resource 13's filtered-vs-smoothed rule.

7. **Risk limits in the executor only.** `max_position_per_market`, `max_order_size`, per-strategy capital caps and daily-loss limits are enforced in the order-placement path with their own tests; the signal layer may propose, the executor disposes. Stop logic for any directional position is placed as a resting order at the venue when the venue supports it; otherwise the executor polls at a bounded interval and the bound is part of the risk model.

8. **Per-strategy capital isolation.** Each accepted strategy gets a `capital_cap` and the executor tracks exposure per strategy; a new child's strategy starts at a cap proportional to its incubation evidence (e.g. 1% of capital per 100 incubation trades at pass, bounded).

9. **Diversification as a measured object.** Keep a rolling correlation matrix of per-strategy daily P&L; report the effective number of independent strategies `N_eff = (Σλ_i)² / Σλ_i²` from its eigenvalues (resource 10's PCA). A proposal to add a strategy whose P&L correlates above 0.7 with an incumbent is scored as a parameter change to the incumbent, not as a new edge.

10. **Promotional-source handling.** Add a `provenance.incentive` field to knowledge records (`independent`, `vendor`, `vendor-with-product-in-loop`). Claims from the last class enter as hypotheses with zero prior weight; only mechanisms (rules, formulas, failure modes) are adopted, never numbers.

11. **Failure-mode checklist the gate must be able to test, drawn from this genre.** Overfitting of parameters (optimizer sweeps: require walk-forward or combinatorial purged CV; report PBO = share of CSCV splits where the in-sample winner lands in the bottom half out-of-sample); selection across many candidates (DSR with the harness's trial count; Harvey–Liu-style haircut on t-stats); look-ahead/repainting (decision-time timestamps, item 6); survivorship and instrument selection (universe must be fixed before the search: altcoins that trended are not a universe); regime dependence (holdout windows must include at least one adverse regime; report performance by regime); fees and slippage (item 1); small live samples (minimum trade counts, item 2); leverage and compounding in reported returns (report un-levered, un-compounded per-trade statistics alongside the equity curve); metric gaming (PF with few trades; win rate with wide stops: require the quartet); execution mismatch (alert latency, partial fills: simulate from the book, not the mid); LLM test-window leakage (item 4: incubation on data that did not exist at selection time is the only clean test).

12. **Ledger as scoreboard.** The ledger already records every proposal, decision and rollback. Add the live P&L stream per strategy, including losses, open exposure and the monitor's band breaches, and make the public-facing summary (`mandel/viz`) draw from the ledger, not from a curated list, so Mandel's "scoreboard" is the thing the creator promises and cannot be shown to deliver.
