## What it says

Legend: **[video page]** = verified from Essentia's page for this video; **[contemporaneous]** = Mossbridge's published position in the same period; **[primary]** = from the underlying papers she relies on.

1. **Thesis of the interview [video page].** Mainstream science "still tends to dismiss extrasensory phenomena (ESP)", but Mossbridge argues the anomalies are informative about the nature of reality, and the conversation explicitly connects her lab results to free will, the nature of time and the mind-body problem. Essentia publishes this because it holds (its About page) that "metaphysical idealism or nondualism—the notion that nature is essentially mental" is the best current model, and it states it is "not philosophically neutral".

2. **Definitions [contemporaneous, JAEX 2023; IONS blog].** *Precognition*: "the human ability to accurately predict future events that are not already predictable based on information about the past or from the five senses." *Premonition*: a feeling-level subset ("a feeling or sense about a future event"). *Presentiment* (= predictive anticipatory activity, PAA): "a physiological form of precognition" whose contents "are usually completely unconscious", operating 1-10 s before a randomly chosen stimulus. In the 2014 paper PAA is defined as "statistically reliable differences between physiological measures recorded seconds before an unpredictable emotional event" and described as "a time-reversed reflection of the usual physiological response."

3. **The canonical experiment [primary, 2012/2014].** Participant sits wired for skin conductance / heart rate / EEG / pupil / BOLD. A hardware true-RNG picks, trial by trial and *with replacement*, an arousing or a neutral stimulus, selected "just before presentation". Dependent variable: the standardized mean difference in the pre-stimulus window between trials that will be arousing and trials that will be neutral, d = (mean_pre|arousing − mean_pre|neutral) / pooled SD. The inferential logic: there must be a clear *post*-stimulus difference for the design to be interpretable; the hypothesis is that an "upstream" (pre) difference anticipates the "downstream" (post) difference. Baseline correction and "clamping" to a pre-stimulus point are standard and contested (see Evidence quality).

4. **The headline numbers [primary, Mossbridge, Tressoldi & Utts 2012].** 26 reports, 7 labs, 1978-2010 (49 found, 23 excluded; estimated ~12 labs in the field). Fixed-effects ES 0.21 (95% CI 0.15-0.27), z = 6.9, p < 2.7e-12; random-effects ES 0.21 (0.13-0.29), z = 5.3. Heterogeneity I² = 27.4, Q = 34.4, p > 0.098. Classical fail-safe N = 265; Orwin fail-safe N = 87 (trivial ES 0.05, missing-study mean 0.001). Trim-and-fill imputed 4 missing negative studies; adjusted ES 0.18 (fixed) / 0.17 (random). Electrodermal-only subset (N = 20): 0.17 (0.096-0.24). Quality score = peer_review + 0.75 × expectation_bias_analysis + 0.5 × randomization; higher-quality half ES 0.24 vs lower half 0.19 (not significantly different, r(quality, ES) = 0.21). Authors' own words: the effect is "small but statistically significant, seems not to be due to expectation bias, and is unlikely to be due to publication bias", yet "we are not at all clear about what explains it."

5. **The update she cites [primary, Duggan & Tressoldi 2018, F1000Research].** 19 new studies / 27 experiments / 36 effect sizes (2008-2018; the paper's own counts are internally inconsistent). Multilevel random ES 0.28 (0.18-0.38); Bayesian estimate identical. I² jumped to 81.9 (from 27.4). Excluding Mossbridge's and Tressoldi's own studies: 0.22 (0.05-0.39). Preregistered 0.31 (0.18-0.45) vs non-preregistered 0.24 (0.08-0.41), overlapping. Copas selection model: 0.28 (0.20-0.36), "no apparent statistical publication bias". Conclusion: presentiment "may hence be considered among the more reliable" psi phenomena.

6. **Behavioral precognition numbers she cites [primary, Bem et al. 2015].** 90 experiments, 33 labs, 14 countries, 12,406 participants: Hedges' g = 0.09 (0.06-0.11), z ≈ 6.3-6.4, p = 1.2e-10, BF ≈ 5e9. Independent (non-Bem) replications: g = 0.06 (0.03-0.09), z = 4.16. "Fast-thinking" protocols g = 0.11 vs "slow-thinking" g = 0.03 (ns). Eight of nine bias tests pass; PET is the exception (0.01, CI includes zero). Mossbridge 2023 notes Bem-type effects "are less robust than for presentiment."

7. **Two-phenomenon taxonomy [contemporaneous, JAEX 2023, Table 1].** She argues presentiment and *precognitive remote viewing* are "distinct phenomena" and tabulates their characteristics: mostly conscious? (No / Yes); prediction up to years ahead? (No / Yes); gender influences accuracy? (Probably / No); feedback required? (Yes / No); interesting targets help? (No / Yes); self-transcendence helps? (Not clear / Yes). Methodological rule she derives: "human capabilities with sharply different characteristics are likely to be served by at least partially separate mechanisms."

8. **The "time loop" hint about mechanism [contemporaneous, JAEX 2023].** In her EEG work the pre-responses "predicted the participants' responses but did not predict the stimulus types themselves." Pre-stimulus activity tends to "match or predict post-responses, rather than ... the nature of the stimuli", which "is almost like a 'time loop'": the organism appears to pre-respond to *its own future state*, not to the external event. She reports a random-forest classifier predicting the upcoming random stimulus from EEG ~550 ms before onset (some measures p = 2.5e-6, 40 subjects).

9. **Model 1, physical time symmetry (PTS) [contemporaneous].** For short-lead, unconscious effects: "presentiment and other short time-frame, largely nonconscious precognitive phenomena act through physical time symmetry in the three dimensions of space and one dimension of time, within the human body and brain." Consciousness is not required; a future post-stimulus response is. Predicted signatures: effect requires feedback/post-response; decays with lead time because physiological pre-responses "would get exponentially noisier the greater the distance between the pre-response and the future event"; may show gender/hormone modulation. She relates PTS to Bierman's CIRTS and Sheehan & Cyrus's thermodynamic retrocausal model. In the 2015 reply: "We do not propose that these responses reverse the arrow of time, but rather that they may reflect time-symmetric processes that are known to be an inherent part of physical reality at the microscopic scale."

10. **Model 2, pervasive universal consciousness (PUC) [contemporaneous].** For long-lead, conscious effects (remote viewing, precognitive dreams): information "arises from a universal consciousness ... an information-rich and pervasive nonlocal, non-3D, non-physical 'field' not confined to an individual's local position in spacetime." Intention is the request channel; electromagnetic radiation is speculated to be the "translator between universal consciousness and local spacetime." She labels this "very speculative" and "a toy model". This is the point of contact with Essentia's idealism and with the user's "informational substrate" framing.

11. **Her falsification program [contemporaneous].** PTS is challenged if pre-responses occur with *no* stimulus ever shown (no feedback, no post-response); test with anesthesia in a single-trial design (PTS predicts pre-response wherever a post-response exists, regardless of awareness). PUC is challenged if no-feedback remote-viewing results are shown to be flawed or if feedback trials prove more accurate; if accuracy for a loved person vs a stranger does not differ; if electromagnetic/biophoton measures never covary with session accuracy; if performance does not depend on intentionality. Her stated standard: "Each model has testable elements and is therefore falsifiable."

12. **Epistemic stance toward skeptics [contemporaneous].** "We don't know how precognition works. What we know is that statistically, on average, people unconsciously can get information about future events" (Nov 2024). Skeptics "don't know how rigorous these studies are, don't read the literature." In the 2015 reply she reverses the burden: "the burden is now on the shoulders of researchers who make assumptions about the temporal nature of non-conscious processing without actually testing those assumptions."

13. **Time and free will [video page topic; contemporaneous wording].** The interview explicitly addresses "free will" and "the nature of time". Her published imagery: "I think of each of the events of our life like beads on a necklace"; "controlled precognition is like making the necklace, making the connection between our past and future selves." She distinguishes a loop-back route (future experience shaping earlier physiology/choice) from a retrieval route (information accessed without later living the exact event). No verified statement about whether she thinks the future is fixed.

14. **Applied forecasting claims [contemporaneous].** 2014: "PAA-sensing tools" (PAASTs) that would "emit an alert 10-20 s before the detonation of an IED", with majority pooling ("if three of four soldiers have their PAAST activated, all four soldiers would take cover"), and "non-linear machine-learning methods" to learn each person's PAA signature. 2021 SPR talk: "Pre-Emptive Intelligence: Precognition as a Forecasting Tool"; she calls her focus "informational time travel". Premonition Code website (true-RNG targets): 682 unpaid participants at chance overall, "interesting" targets above chance (p = 0.0007); a 307-participant free-response study above chance (p = 0.002). She notes practitioners who "predict targets associated with financial markets claim that feedback is crucial" (associative remote viewing, Katz et al.). Choice Compass app reads heart rate while a user considers a choice.

15. **State modulators [contemporaneous].** Self-transcendence and "unconditional love" are claimed to raise accuracy on conscious free-response precognition (Roe et al. 2020; her own post-hoc then pre-registered analyses); for presentiment the effect of meditators is unclear and may reverse because meditators suppress arousal. Positive Precog community principles: respect for the unknown, ethics, accuracy, compassion, honesty.

16. **Methodological recommendations she endorses [primary, 2012/2014/2015 reply].** Register experiments in advance; use hardware RNGs and sample with replacement; use sensory-rich stimuli and long inter-stimulus delays; prefer single-trial between-participant designs that "cannot be due to trial order or traditional expectation bias"; run expectation-bias analyses (regress pre-stimulus activity on the run length of preceding neutral trials); report both pre- and post-stimulus windows; replicate across labs with agreed protocols.

## Evidence quality

**Rating: speculative** (for the central claim that physiology or cognition carries information about randomly determined future events). The statistical apparatus around the claim is formal and the primary meta-analysis is a competent worked example, but the inferential leap is extraordinary and the discriminating experiments are largely undone. Reasons:

- *Source type.* An interview produced by a foundation that states it is "not philosophically neutral" and exists to promote idealism. Treat the framing as advocacy even where the numbers are accurate.
- *Effect size regime.* d ≈ 0.2 on physiology, g ≈ 0.09 on behavior. Many-labs small effects are exactly the signature produced by either a small real effect or a small systematic bias (analytic flexibility, selection, confounds). The data alone cannot distinguish these; design can, and the decisive designs (pre-registered, adversarial, single-trial between-subjects, no-feedback) are few.
- *Independent replication of the behavioral strand failed.* Ritchie, Wiseman & French 2012: three pre-registered replications of Bem's retroactive recall, combined n = 150, p = 0.83. Galak et al. 2012: seven studies, N = 3,289, d = 0.04, not different from zero; "the only moderator that yields significantly different results is whether the experiment was conducted by Bem or not." The 2015 meta-analysis' 90 experiments still show g = 0.09, with "slow-thinking" protocols at zero.
- *Specific methodological critiques (Schwarzkopf 2014) that remain live.* (a) Several included studies are of "questionable quality", one fMRI study "commits major errors with multiple comparison correction and circular inference"; (b) the ~2:1 neutral:arousing imbalance means "an attentive participant will quickly learn statistical properties of the sequence"; (c) the standard expectation-bias check assumes effects "scale linearly with expectation", a "gambler's fallacy"-type assumption, and "cannot possibly detect whether participants learn statistical regularities"; (d) baseline "clamping" can "inadvertently introduce artifacts in the pre-stimulus period"; (e) biologically, measures with different latencies cannot all reflect "the same precognitive neural event" and "Why should blood oxygenation increase before neural activity?"; (f) the hypothesis "does not lead to any falsifiable hypothesis" and post-hoc quantum speculation is "insufficient unless they can make testable predictions."
- *Proponents' rebuttals are partial.* Mossbridge et al. 2015 show by simulation that with ~35 participants per study the linear expectation-bias test misses ~8% of bias-induced false positives (so ~92% of the 19 studies that ran the test remain "unexplained"), argue that imbalance biases expectation in the *wrong* direction to produce the effect, and note that only 2 of 14 mainstream labs shared data. These are reasonable, but they do not supply the pre-registered adversarial replication that would settle it, and the 2023 review still describes the mechanism models as "toy" and "very speculative".
- *Internal inconsistencies and heterogeneity in the update.* Duggan & Tressoldi 2018 report mismatched study counts (19 vs 26 studies; 4 vs 14 preregistered), I² rising from 27 to 82 (a sign that different phenomena or analysis pipelines are being pooled), data-sharing refusals explained only as "confidential", and a reviewer who called p-hacking/multiple comparisons "by far the most serious concern."
- *Post-hoc subsets.* "Interesting targets predicted better (p = 0.0007)" while the full sample is at chance, and lunar-phase or gender moderators found post hoc, are textbook forking paths until pre-registered and replicated.
- *What is genuinely solid.* The community's methodological toolkit (true RNGs, sampling with replacement, pre/post symmetric analysis, fail-safe N, trim-and-fill, Copas, p-curve, permutation tests respecting autocorrelation, quality weighting declared in advance) is sound and transferable. The sociological observation that fields converge on small positive effects under weak controls is itself a useful lesson.

Separate verdict on the framing: Essentia's idealism and the PUC "universal consciousness as information store" are metaphysical positions, not mechanisms. They make no quantitative prediction Mandel could score, so they carry no evidential weight for substrate design.

## What Mandel adopts

- **Symmetric pre/post lead-lag test.** Before claiming any signal "anticipates" an event, require (i) a clear post-event response in the same channel and (ii) a pre-event differential measured with the same statistic, (iii) under a permutation null that preserves autocorrelation. Because: this is Mossbridge's "upstream must mirror downstream" logic stripped of metaphysics, and it is exactly how a lead indicator for a market move should be certified.
- **Order-effect / expectation-bias covariate as mandatory.** Regress any apparent anticipation on run length and time-since-last-event; if the "anticipation" is explained, it is sequence learning, not signal. Because: the gambler's-fallacy confound Schwarzkopf raised is identical to the confound in mean-reversion and streak-based trading features.
- **Pre-registration before results, enforced by the ledger.** Mossbridge's own camp now recommends it; the F1000 reviewer accepted the update only after a preregistration split. Because: Mandel's hash-chained ledger already encodes "criterion first, result second"; this resource shows what happens to a field that adopted it late.
- **Internal file-drawer accounting.** Log every experiment variant Mandel or a child runs (not just the promoted ones), and compute fail-safe-N / p-curve / Copas-style checks on Mandel's own experiment population. Because: a self-improving system that tries many variants is its own publication-bias generator; the Deflated Sharpe Ratio already in `validation/backtest_eval.py` needs the true trial count from a registry, not a self-report.
- **Characteristic-table taxonomy before modeling.** Mirror JAEX Table 1: for each candidate signal record lead-time range, decay-with-horizon, explicit vs latent, feedback-required, state-dependence. Because: "sharply different characteristics are likely served by partially separate mechanisms" is good engineering; different signal classes need different validators and different horizons.
- **Kill criterion on every hypothesis.** Each model she proposes names what would refute it. Because: a hypothesis without a kill criterion cannot be gated; Mandel should refuse to pre-register one.
- **Endogenous vs exogenous target distinction.** Her "pre-responses predict the participant's own response, not the stimulus" is a warning: a signal may be predicting the system's *own* future action (reflexive) rather than the world. Because: a trading signal that forecasts the bot's own order flow or a child's own future call is leakage, not edge.
- **Horizon-indexed skill.** PTS predicts exponential decay of signal with lead time. Because: a source whose Brier skill does *not* decay with horizon is more likely leaking resolution information than seeing the future.
- **Pooling only after independence.** Her 3-of-4 majority-vote PAAST idea is an ensemble of weak detectors. Because: it is only valid if detector errors are independent and each detector is calibrated; Mandel must estimate residual correlation among children before voting.
- **Separate evidence from framing in every knowledge record.** Because: this resource is the clearest case where accurate numbers travel inside a metaphysical wrapper; the two must be scored differently.
- **Adversarial collaboration as a role.** Because: the Schwarzkopf exchange produced more methodological clarity than a decade of proponent-only meta-analysis; Mandel should keep a standing "skeptic" pass (or a child with that role) in its gate.

## What Mandel holds loosely or rejects

- **Reject** precognition/retrocausal information as a design basis for any forecasting or trading component. Even on proponents' numbers the signal is d ≈ 0.2 on noisy physiology or g ≈ 0.09 on behavior, is unreplicated out of sample by independent groups, and has never been shown to yield out-of-sample profit under pre-registration. Expected value of building on it is negative after costs and research time.
- **Reject** the burden-shifting argument ("the burden is now on the skeptics"). Prior plausibility is legitimately asymmetric for claims that would require new physics; Mandel's gate should weight priors, not pretend neutrality.
- **Hold loosely** the claim that presentiment is "among the more reliable" psi phenomena. The claim rests on proponent-conducted meta-analyses with rising heterogeneity, inconsistent counts and limited data sharing.
- **Hold loosely** post-hoc moderator findings (interesting targets, gender, lunar phase, unconditional love). Treat as hypotheses awaiting pre-registered replication, not results.
- **Reject** the PUC model and Essentia's idealism as operational components. Mandel is substrate-agnostic: its "informational substrate" is versioned files, a ledger and evaluation harnesses, not a nonlocal field. Record the frame as context, not mechanism.
- **Reject** anecdotal cases (lottery dreams, premonitions) as evidence. Base rates and selection make them uninformative.
- **Hold loosely** associative remote viewing for markets. The practitioner literature is small, feedback-dependent by its own account and not independently replicated; do not allocate capital or children's time to it.
- **Hold loosely** the fail-safe N as reassurance. Large fail-safe N is expected whenever many small positive studies are pooled; it does not address analytic flexibility within studies, which is the live concern.

## Design implications for the RSI predictive substrate

Concrete additions, phrased against the existing `mandel/` modules (`forecasts.py`, `ledger.py`, `validation/scoring.py`, `validation/backtest_eval.py`).

**Data structures**
- `Hypothesis` record (new, in the knowledge store): `{id, claim, signal_class, target_type: "exogenous"|"endogenous", lead_time_window: [min_s, max_s], expected_decay: "none"|"linear"|"exponential", feedback_required: bool, kill_criterion: str, analysis_plan_hash, preregistered_at}`. Registering a hypothesis without `kill_criterion` and `analysis_plan_hash` is a ledger-level error.
- `Experiment` record (new): `{hypothesis_id, variant_params, data_window, result_stats, promoted: bool}` appended for *every* run, promoted or not. This is Mandel's anti-file-drawer table.
- Extend `Forecast` (`forecasts.py`) with `horizon_s: float` (already has free-text `horizon`) and `target_type`, so skill can be bucketed by lead time and reflexive forecasts are separable.
- Extend `SourceScore` with `by_horizon: dict[bucket, {n, brier, bss}]` and `decay_slope` (BSS regressed on log horizon).
- `ClaimRow` in every knowledge file: `{effect, n, es, ci, independence: "proponent"|"independent"|"adversarial", replication_status, framing}` so evidence and framing never share a field.

**Loops**
- *Lead-lag certification loop* for any candidate indicator S and event class E: (1) verify post-event differential Δ_post = mean(S | after E_arousing) − mean(S | after E_neutral) under a circular-shift permutation null (preserves autocorrelation, as Radin's permutation approach did); (2) only if Δ_post is significant, compute Δ_pre with the identical window length and statistic; (3) regress Δ_pre on run-length and time-since-last-event; (4) certify only if Δ_pre survives (1)-(3) on a held-out walk-forward split with embargo (`walk_forward_splits`). Ledger events: `hypothesis.preregistered` → `experiment.run` (one per variant) → `gate.decision`.
- *Internal meta-analysis loop* (periodic): pool `Experiment` rows per hypothesis; compute I² across children/variants, Orwin fail-safe N = k × (ES_obs − ES_trivial) / (ES_trivial − ES_missing), p-curve on promoted results; flag any hypothesis whose promoted-subset ES exceeds its all-runs ES by more than the block-bootstrap CI (`block_bootstrap_pvalue`) as "selection-inflated".
- *Adversarial pass*: for every proposal, run the identical pipeline on label-shuffled and synthetic-random-walk data; adoption requires the effect to vanish under both nulls. The pass is a required ledger event before `gate.decision`.
- *Heterogeneity alarm*: when pooling children's results, a jump in I² (as in 27 → 82 between the 2012 and 2018 meta-analyses) blocks pooling and triggers a taxonomy split (re-tag signals by `signal_class`).

**Validation rules**
- No adoption without a pre-registered kill criterion that was *not* met.
- Deflated Sharpe Ratio (`deflated_sharpe_ratio`) must take `n_trials` from the `Experiment` registry count for that hypothesis, never from the proposer.
- Any source whose `decay_slope` is ≥ 0 (skill not falling with horizon) is quarantined pending a leakage audit.
- Any `target_type == "endogenous"` forecast is excluded from `skill_vs_market` and from promotion decisions.
- Minimum detectable effect and power must be stated at registration; in the d ≈ 0.2 regime the simulation in the 2015 reply (~8% undetected false positives at n ≈ 35) is the cautionary benchmark; use it to set minimum n per experiment.
- Evidence weighting schemes (analogues of the quality score peer_review + 0.75·expectation_bias + 0.5·randomization) must be registered before the evidence is seen; post-hoc weighting is a ledger violation.

**Metrics**
- Per-source, per-horizon Brier, log loss, ECE and Brier skill vs market (`validation/scoring.py`), plus `decay_slope`.
- Experiment-registry integrity: `promoted_fraction`, `promoted_vs_all_ES_gap`, `internal_I2`, `orwin_fail_safe_N`, `p_curve_right_skew`.
- Independence of pooled detectors: mean pairwise residual correlation among children; k-of-n voting admitted only when it is below a registered threshold and each member is individually calibrated on held-out data.
- Null-pass survival: fraction of a proposal's claimed effect that remains under shuffled labels (target ≈ 0).
