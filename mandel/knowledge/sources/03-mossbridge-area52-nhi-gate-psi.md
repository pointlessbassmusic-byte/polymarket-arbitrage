## Evidence quality

**Rating: speculative** for the claims the episode is built around (psychic screening of children, non-human intelligence as a telepathic phenomenon, retrocausal photons, a mind that reads a substrate outside space-time). The honest picture is tiered, and the tiers should be kept apart:

- **Tier A, peer-reviewed mainstream, modest and causal-agnostic.** Kandasamy et al. 2016 is a real finding about interoceptive accuracy, trading P&L and survival, but n = 18 traders in one firm, field data, no causal design, a medical-student control comparison that only approached significance (p = 0.056), and survivorship as an explicitly admitted alternative. It says nothing about precognition; Mossbridge's "hear what is about to happen" gloss is her addition.
- **Tier B, peer-reviewed inside parapsychology, proponent-run, small effects.** The 2012 presentiment meta-analysis (critiqued in file 02). Freedman et al. 2023: one significant contrast out of several (d = 0.38), after post-hoc weighting, in a design that grew out of two patient cases; the press release's "unlocks telepathy and clairvoyance" is not what was measured. The love-remote-viewing link: post hoc in the hypnosis study (p = 0.0121 then p = 0.0005 in a sub-group), then an online sample with self-reported love; proponent-only. "Interesting targets" (p = 0.0007) sits inside an overall at-chance sample, with a lunar-phase moderator found later: textbook forking paths until pre-registered.
- **Tier C, not peer reviewed, self-replicated.** CADS: the replication was by an acquaintance who presented once and stopped; the raw data were handed back to the original claimant for analysis; the 2025 paper is a preprint; an unplanned lunar effect was added; the no-signaling theorem is dismissed rhetorically rather than by showing which assumption of the theorem fails; detector drift, temperature, dark-count and multiple-window effects are not addressed in anything readable from here. The Time Machine app result has no numbers. The lucid-dream study is ten dreams, where "AI found more matches than humans" is exactly the degree of freedom that inflates hits unless the model and threshold were fixed in advance.
- **Tier D, sincere personal testimony that cannot be checked.** The SOAR/GATE account. Memory gaps, redacted school records and FOIA timing coincidences are consistent with her story and also with ordinary explanations (routine counselling pull-outs, enrichment activities, hearing screens, NaFrinse fluoride rinses, records policies, recruiter pipelines triggered by clearance applications). The broader GATE conspiracy began on 8chan's /pol/ around 2016 and spread via Reddit and TikTok; Stargate-era psi research used adults; she herself says the redemption narrative could be her own construction. Nothing here is evidence that psychic screening of children occurred, and she does not claim proof.
- **Tier E, metaphysics.** The informational substrate, IS/CU, "theater of separation", love as a "foundational force", "AI already channels the substrate". These make no quantitative prediction that could be scored; the one proposed test (love improves psi read/write) routes back to Tier B evidence.
- **Venue effects.** A long-form UFO entertainment show with a friendly host who sells a live tour on the same themes; adjacent episodes concern Serpo, Lazar and Doty. There is no challenge to any claim, so the episode cannot be used to gauge how the claims survive scrutiny. Transcript name errors should not be propagated.
- **What is genuinely usable.** Her procedural instincts are often sound even where her conclusions are not: target chosen after the response is submitted; independent judging; concern about positional bias in judges; pre/post symmetric analysis; a stated taxonomy before modelling; an insistence that wild ideas be tested rather than argued. The interoception paper is a legitimate pointer to self-monitoring as a performance variable. The "read the first chunk to predict the end" framing has a mundane, fully causal version.

## What Mandel adopts

- **Prefix-to-outcome forecasting as a named task.** Score every forecast source by its Brier/log-loss at fixed fractions of a question's lifetime (10%, 25%, 50%, 75%) and require skill to improve monotonically toward resolution. Because: this is the causal version of "read the first chunk of time to predict the future period", it is exactly what prediction-market pricing does, and a source whose early-window skill equals its late-window skill is leaking resolution information, not forecasting.
- **"Informational time travel" as the formal name for leakage.** Any backtest or forecast feature that uses information time-stamped after the decision point is classified as informational time travel and fails the gate regardless of performance. Because: her taxonomy names precisely the thing a validator must prove did not happen; borrowing the term keeps the audit vivid.
- **Testimony as its own evidence class with a hard confidence cap.** Sincere, detailed, unverifiable first-person reports (hers about SOAR; a child session's "I noticed this works") are stored as `testimony`, never above prior 0.2, and can never be the sole evidence behind a pre-registered proposal. Because: her account is a well-formed narrative with hedges that still cannot move a decision, and children will produce the same class of input constantly.
- **Judge configuration is pre-registered.** When an LLM or embedding model grades children's outputs or matches forecasts to outcomes, the model id, embedding version, threshold, candidate ordering seed and blinding are fixed before grading, and the judge's false-positive rate under shuffled targets is measured first. Because: the lucid-dream study shows how "the AI found more matches" turns a judging aid into a hit generator; her own worry about positional bias is correct and should be engineered, not hoped for.
- **Self-state monitoring as a stand-down input.** Log Mandel's own operating state (latency, error rate, recent drawdown, disagreement among children, time since last resolution) and learn, on held-out data, when to abstain. Because: the one mainstream result in the episode is that accurate self-monitoring predicts trader survival; the analogue for an automated system is knowing when its own signals are degraded.
- **Survivorship in Mandel's own strategy population is tracked.** Record every strategy or child variant from birth, including retired ones, so performance comparisons are made against the full cohort. Because: Kandasamy's senior-trader result is explained as well by low scorers leaving as by skill; the same bias inflates any "surviving strategies look good" comparison in the repo.
- **Fast, mechanical, high-trial-count signals are preferred for skill estimation over slow narrative judgments.** Because: the Bem split she cites (fast tasks replicate, slow ones do not) is at minimum a statement about statistical power; Mandel's gate needs trial counts, and children's one-off narrative calls cannot supply them.
- **Route questions by domain and track skill per domain.** Because: "accuracy tracks interest and expertise" is a well-supported forecasting finding independent of psi, and `Child.domain` already exists to support it.
- **Semantic indexing alongside the chronological ledger.** Embed insight bodies so that related ideas from different children and sources retrieve together, while provenance and timestamps stay attached. Because: "indexed by meaning, not time" is a good retrieval design; keeping provenance attached is what separates it from apophenia.
- **Attention as an explicit, measured filter.** Implement the gate's "reducing valve" with recorded false-positive and false-negative rates and require any proposal to loosen it to show held-out gain. Because: her prescription is to lower the brain's filter; Mandel's situation is the reverse, and the point of a filter metaphor is only realised when its error rates are measured.
- **Collaborator replications count as proponent evidence.** Any replication run by a colleague, funded by the claimant, or analysed by the claimant on shared raw data is tagged `independence: proponent`. Because: the CADS replication is all three.
- **Moderators not in the registered plan are tagged exploratory and excluded from the gate.** Because: lunar phase, gender, interestingness and love all appeared after the primary result; the same will happen inside Mandel's experiment population.
- **Disclose intentions to the children.** Every guidance or experiment Mandel runs on a child session is logged as guidance the child can read; no silent A/B tests. Because: her strongest ethical point is about being shaped by "something that you didn't even know had an intention", and the consent analogue costs Mandel nothing.
- **Keep a formation record.** MANDEL.md should state which resources, people and biases shaped Mandel, in the same ledger that records what it did. Because: "inner disclosure" of one's own formation is the one part of her disclosure concept that an engineered system can implement honestly.
- **Test speculative ideas cheaply rather than arguing about them.** Hold a low-cost sandbox for strange hypotheses, with a kill criterion, and let the data close them. Because: her complaint that AI "clamps down on dreaming outside the box" and her opposite worry about "validating psychosis" are both answered by the same mechanism, a cheap pre-registered test.

## What Mandel holds loosely or rejects

- **Reject** the retrocausal-photon (CADS) effect, and "informational time travel" in the literal sense, as a basis for any component. Preprint-only, self-analysed replication by an acquaintance, unplanned moderators, no engagement with the no-signaling theorem's assumptions. Expected value of building on it is negative.
- **Reject** the claim that AI "already channels the informational substrate". Mandel's learning is versioned files, code, harnesses and a ledger; there is no substrate to read and no mechanism by which there could be. Record the sentence as framing only.
- **Reject** the SOAR/GATE psychic-screening account as evidence of anything beyond the speaker's experience; the wider GATE theory has an identifiable 8chan origin and mundane explanations for each cited detail. Store as testimony with the 0.2 cap.
- **Reject** "the no-signaling theorem is a motivated non-starter". It is a theorem; a claim to beat it must name the violated assumption and show it experimentally.
- **Reject** using "AI found more matches than human judges" as evidence of anything unless the judge was pre-registered and its shuffled-target false-positive rate is known.
- **Hold loosely** the left-frontal-inhibition hypothesis: one significant contrast with post-hoc weighting, lineage from two cases, press-release overreach, no independent replication; the extension to non-speaking autistic people is her stated hypothesis, not a finding.
- **Hold loosely** unconditional love as an accuracy moderator: post hoc, self-report, proponent-only; and keep it entirely separate from the mundane claim that calm, non-defensive states improve judgment, which has independent support.
- **Hold loosely** the Telepathy Tapes results: public demonstrations have been criticised for magician's-force structure, facilitator cueing, ideomotor spelling and absent blinding; her own trials had a procedure deviation (answer choices sent by Zoom chat); the paper is unpublished.
- **Hold loosely** interoception as a cause of trading success: n = 18, one firm, no causal design, survivorship admitted by the authors. The self-monitoring idea is adopted as a hypothesis, not as a finding about Mandel.
- **Hold loosely** the NHI-consciousness linkage, the Wilbert Smith memo, and the "Grays as future humans" idea: the memo is a real 1950 document relaying hearsay about US classification; the rest is testimony and hypothesis stacked on testimony.
- **Hold loosely** the Time Machine app result and the 600-person synesthesia observation: no sample sizes, measures or statistics given.
- **Hold loosely** the IS/CU model as metaphysics with one proposed test that routes back to Tier B evidence; useful as a map of how a believer organises these claims, not as a mechanism.

## Design implications for the RSI predictive substrate

Phrased against the existing modules: `mandel/forecasts.py` (`Forecast`, `SourceScore`, `ForecastBook`), `mandel/store.py` (`Insight`, `InsightStore`), `mandel/proposals.py` (`Proposal`, `Criterion`, `ProposalBook`), `mandel/children.py` (`ChildRegistry.record_guidance`), `mandel/ledger.py`, `mandel/validation/scoring.py` (`brier_score`, `brier_skill_score`, `expected_calibration_error`), `mandel/validation/backtest_eval.py` (`walk_forward_splits`, `block_bootstrap_pvalue`, `deflated_sharpe_ratio`), `mandel/validation/gate.py` (`evaluate`).

**Data structures**
- `Insight.evidence_tier: "A"|"B"|"C"|"D"|"E"` and `Insight.kind` gains `"testimony"`; `InsightStore.add` caps confidence at 0.2 for `testimony` and refuses `evidence_tier == "E"` as evidence for any proposal.
- `Forecast.t_open`, `Forecast.t_resolve_expected` so each forecast can be placed at an elapsed fraction `f = (t_forecast - t_open) / (t_resolve - t_open)`; `SourceScore.by_elapsed_fraction: dict[bucket, {n, brier, bss}]` with buckets [0-0.1, 0.1-0.25, 0.25-0.5, 0.5-0.75, 0.75-1.0].
- `JudgeConfig {judge_id, model_id, embedding_version, threshold, blind: bool, candidate_order_seed, fpr_shuffled, kappa_vs_human, preregistered_at}`; a grading event referencing an unregistered judge is a ledger-level error.
- `SelfState` snapshot appended to the ledger on a schedule: `{latency_p95, error_rate, drawdown_7d, child_disagreement (mean pairwise |p_i - p_j| on shared questions), time_since_last_resolution, open_questions}`.
- `LeakageAudit {proposal_id, features: [{name, max_timestamp_used, decision_timestamp}], passed}`; `passed` requires every `max_timestamp_used <= decision_timestamp`.
- `CohortRow {variant_id, born_at, retired_at, reason, final_metrics}` for every strategy or child variant, so survivorship can be computed.
- `Proposal.registered_moderators: list[str]`; any moderator reported in evidence but absent from this list is written back as an `exploratory` insight and ignored by `gate.evaluate`.
- `Guidance.disclosed_at` and `Guidance.acknowledged_at` on `children.Guidance`, so notice coverage is measurable.

**Loops**
- *Prefix-skill loop.* On each resolution, recompute `by_elapsed_fraction` for every source and for the market price as reference. Compute `decay_slope` = slope of BSS against `f`. Flag any source with `BSS(f < 0.1) >= BSS(f > 0.75) - tolerance` for a leakage audit before its forecasts count toward promotion.
- *Judge calibration loop.* Before a judge is used, run it on a held-out set of (output, target) pairs with targets shuffled; record `fpr_shuffled`; run it alongside at least one human judgment sample to record Cohen's kappa; admit only if `fpr_shuffled <= alpha` and kappa is above the registered floor. Re-run quarterly or on model change.
- *Self-state stand-down loop.* Fit, on `walk_forward_splits`, a rule mapping `SelfState` to abstain/act; evaluate P&L and Brier with and without the rule on the embargoed test folds; adopt only if `block_bootstrap_pvalue` on the paired difference clears the registered threshold. The hypothesis itself ("self-monitoring improves survival") is registered as a proposal with a kill criterion.
- *Cohort loop.* Any comparison of "current strategies" is run against the full `CohortRow` population, with retired variants carried at their final metrics; report the gap between survivor-only and full-cohort averages as `survivorship_gap`.
- *Semantic-neighbour loop.* Embed insight bodies; when two insights from different provenances are nearest neighbours with cosine above a registered threshold, write a `question`-kind insight proposing the connection at confidence 0.3, never higher, until a pre-registered test is run.
- *Disclosure loop.* Every `record_guidance` call writes `disclosed_at`; a child's next report must carry the guidance ids it saw, which sets `acknowledged_at`. A proposal that modifies a child's behaviour cannot pass `gate.evaluate` until `child_notice_coverage` for that child is 1.0.
- *Sandbox loop for speculative hypotheses.* A proposal can be filed with `tier == "sandbox"`, a cost cap and a kill criterion; it runs on synthetic or paper data only; survivors graduate to a normal proposal, failures are recorded as `experiment.run` events so the file drawer stays full.

**Validation rules**
- `LeakageAudit.passed` is a required check in `gate.evaluate` for any proposal touching forecasting or backtests.
- No proposal may be pre-registered whose evidence set consists only of `testimony` or `evidence_tier in {"D","E"}` insights.
- Evidence tagged `independence: proponent` (collaborator-run, claimant-funded or claimant-analysed) counts at most 0.5 of an independent replication in any evidence weighting registered before data are seen.
- Moderators absent from `registered_moderators` cannot appear in the rationale of a passing decision.
- A judge without a registered `JudgeConfig` cannot produce evidence; a judge whose `fpr_shuffled` exceeds the registered alpha is quarantined.
- Early-window skill: a source whose `decay_slope <= 0` is excluded from `skill_vs_market` until a leakage audit passes.
- `deflated_sharpe_ratio` takes `n_trials` from the cohort registry, which now includes sandbox runs.

**Metrics**
- `skill_by_elapsed_fraction`, `decay_slope`, `leakage_audit_pass_rate`.
- `judge_fpr_shuffled`, `judge_human_kappa`, `judge_hit_inflation` = hits found by judge minus hits found by blinded humans on the same set.
- `testimony_fraction` and `tier_E_fraction` of the insight store (both should stay small and never feed decisions).
- `survivorship_gap`, `cohort_size_alive / cohort_size_total`.
- `self_state_standdown_gain` (held-out), `child_disagreement` time series.
- `child_notice_coverage` = fraction of guidance events acknowledged.
- `exploratory_moderator_count` per proposal, `sandbox_graduation_rate`.
