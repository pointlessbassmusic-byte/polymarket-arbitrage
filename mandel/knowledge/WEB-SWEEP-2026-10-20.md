## Tested against Mandel's and the children's learnings

Consistency of the 154 candidates with what Mandel and the children already measured: 7 consistent, 88 mixed, 46 contradicted, 13 with no evidence either way. The cross-check recorded 252 specific contradictions (163 from Mandel's verdicts, 73 from the children's findings, 16 from the learning program's results). The pattern is the one the children and Mandel keep finding: published premia decay after publication, liquid markets are calibrated, a lead that exists is too fast for a taker, and what survives is a structural premium (variance risk, carry, flows) or a maker's edge, not a price pattern.

The learning program's own scan (T-579, standing after refutation) adds one contradiction of its own: the literature's intraday momentum into the close appears in 2023-2026 bars as reversal of the return since the session open and of the VWAP distance, in every asset class, too small to pay a taker.

These tensions are now edges in the signal map (SYNTHESIS.md, Tensions), and every market-variable concept carries the tally of Mandel's and the children's verdicts for it (the evidence overlay).
