# Salvage audit, 2026-10-20

The user's question: are any of the killed strategies the inverse of a correct one, and can a working strategy be
derived from a failure (a related variant, a different implementation, a different hold or exit, a risk change), so
that useful parts of a model are not thrown out because one rule built on them lost money?

Method: every not_supported, inconclusive and parked theory (301) was re-read from its latest record in
`state/experiments.jsonl`. For each, the question was where the result came from: the gross signal, the costs, the
exit, the instrument, one side of a two-sided rule, or one asset of a pooled claim. A failure is invertible only when
the inverse rule clears its own costs: if the tested rule earned net = gross - cost, the inverse earns
-gross - cost = -net - 2 x cost, so the loss must be significantly worse than twice the round-trip cost.

Nothing below changes a verdict. Each salvage is a new pre-registered theory (T-544 to T-558, ledger events
`theory.preregistered` with basis `salvage-audit-2026-10-20`), tested on data or a period its parent did not use, or
forward only. The in-sample observation that motivated each one is the reason to register it, not evidence for it.

## 6. What the audit says about the model families

- Prediction-market microstructure (Kalshi and Polymarket): small real gross effects (reversion, convergence) inside
  costs of 3-4 cents a round trip; neither direction pays. The value left is calibration (T-557) and the option-model
  test on the US venue (T-544).
- Options: the model-priced verdicts are not decisions; the quote-priced tests on archived DoltHub chains (T-550 here,
  and the decision-centre review's list of 13 SPY structures) are the efficiency that turns them into decisions.
- Trend and momentum: as return engines they fail after deflation; as drawdown controls they are robust (T-301),
  which is why T-551 combines one with a premium that is supported.
- Carry (crypto basis, funding, gold): the carry is measurably positive in several records (T-197, T-258, T-329,
  T-370); the implementations lost on turnover, settlement measurement or hedge cost, which T-552, T-553 and T-555 test.
